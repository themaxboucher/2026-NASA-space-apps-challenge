# Echoes of Exploration: design and bug audit (14 Nov 2026)

Two independent reviewers went through the site in the browser at desktop (1440×900) and phone (375 px) sizes, read the code, and checked it against Vercel's Web Interface Guidelines. Nothing was changed during the review. Items are ordered by severity inside each section.

## Explore atlas (`/explore`)

### Top five to fix first

1. **Group stacked pins.** At each Apollo site the lander, rover, science station and mirror sit on one spot, so 26 of 38 Moon pins are hidden under another pin and can't be clicked. Show one pin per site with a count ("Apollo 15 · 4") that opens a small list.
2. **Stop the scroll trap on the globe.** The mouse wheel over the globe zooms it instead of scrolling the page, and on phones a swipe spins it (it covers about 520 of 812 px). Zoom with Ctrl/⌘ + wheel or +/− buttons; use `touch-action: pan-y`.
3. **Fix the timeline's year scale.** Axis labels are spread evenly, but the gaps are 11, 10, 10, 10, 10 and 16 years, so "2010" sits about 4 years off. Bars and the year cursor also use slightly different scales. Use one scale for all three, and fewer labels on phones (they currently run together).
4. **Restructure the machine page.** It's a 5,400 px scroll inside a 575 px panel, and "At a glance" is the 4th section. Put the glance tiles right under the status, fold maps and links into expandable sections, and tell each story once (Opportunity's dust storm is told four times).
5. **Don't rely on colour alone for status.** The rust "still working" and brown "gone quiet" colours differ mostly in lightness and fail for colour-blind readers, and brown pins vanish on Mars and the Moon. Add a shape cue (filled dot / hollow ring / mirror symbol) and make "gone quiet" a lighter sand colour.

### Design and UX

- **Deep space zooms into empty black.** Voyager 1 becomes a lone dot with no Sun, scale or way back. Add an inset "you are here" map and a "Whole solar system" button; labels are about 10 px, so give them a minimum size.
- **The ground explorer's controls cover half the view** (two button rows, help text, credit). Put help behind a "?" shown once, one compact control pad, credit behind an "i". Unexplained thin white vertical lines run from the sky to the machines.
- **The intro panel leads with "0 still send data home"** and is mostly empty. Use it for a small stacked bar of what happened to them, which can double as a filter.
- **The roster is collapsed below the timeline** even though the intro says "pick a name from the list below", and the eyebrow says "tap to open" when it's already open. Open it by default on desktop.
- **The Moon timeline** is 42 rows of near-instant dots on a diagonal; a dot strip by year would use far less space. No hover labels on touch.
- Search results start with lowercase mono "the Moon ·".

### Data representation

- Roster group bars repeat the counts written next to them; drop them or use one stacked bar per world.
- Add a "how long a radio hello takes" comparison across the deep-space craft (Moon 1.3 s → Voyager 1 about 24 h). It would be the most memorable chart for kids.
- Deep-space machines have no recorded fate, so their roster is a flat list and the failures bank has no deep-space entries (e.g. Galileo's jammed antenna).
- "From the Sun 172.3 AU" needs a kid comparison ("172× Earth's distance").

### Accessibility

- `aria-live` on the whole machine page reads about 5,000 words on every selection; announce only "Opened Opportunity Rover".
- World tabs lack `aria-controls`, tab panels and arrow-key navigation.
- Search input needs `role="combobox"`, `aria-expanded` and a `name`.
- No skip link; 67 tab stops before the machine page. Make pins and timeline bars one tab stop each with arrow keys.
- Timeline bars are `<b role="button">` (use `<button>`); bars are 6–7 px and pins 14 px tall, under the 24 px minimum hit size.
- `#groundCv{outline:none}` removes focus with no replacement.
- Photos have no width/height (layout shift), and `max-height:300px; object-fit:cover` crops portraits.
- 3D zoom buttons are 30 px; use 44 px on touch.

### Still to do (top five)

1. **Mobile layout.** The intro sheet covers the whole machine, and the Mission file opens at half the screen over the dots. Start the Mission file as a collapsed chip on phones and cap the intro at about 40% height.
2. **Make the lock guide kids.** Pressing locked Next only shakes it. Add a ✓ on found dots, edge-of-screen arrows to unfound ones (Falcon is 130 m away), and a small reward when the last spot is found. The "skip" link is intentionally quiet but its contrast (2.9:1) is below 4.5:1.
3. **Content pass.** Give Cassini and Voyager a "What happened" box (their atlas fate is empty; Cassini also looks intact although it burned up in Saturn). Make fun facts not repeat hotspots and use the narrator's voice (they're in first person with straight quotes). Use the myth-buster that Opportunity's "my battery is low" was a journalist's paraphrase. Add an end-screen recap.
4. **Typography.** Many labels are 10–11 px uppercase monospace (`.nav-sub`, `.trail-label`, stat labels, `.hint`), and the tour mixes three type voices (serif title, wide Saira headings, Plex Mono UI). Use at least 13 px, sentence case, and the same serif display face as the atlas.
5. **Models match the story.** Ingenuity shows full-length blades though all four snapped; Opportunity's "The last message" dot sits on the instruments instead of the high-gain dish; Voyager 1's "Nuclear power" dot floats in empty space (`stops.ts:512`).

### Other bugs and polish

- End screen always says "You visited 6 of the 79 machines", even after skipping or deep-linking; "79" is hardcoded (`Tour.tsx`).
- `/fly` has no Esc handler, and its dots float in a diamond around the craft rather than on parts; Juno has no Jupiter and Dawn no Ceres behind them; every source link just says "Source".
- Cards are 86% opaque, so neighbouring glowing dots show through text. The canvas shows a "grab" cursor although a click now locks the mouse, and there is no "Esc to free the mouse" hint once the intro is closed.
- The name plate and intro card show the same name and status.

### Accessibility

- Trail-dot `<span>`s use `aria-label` without a role, so progress is never announced; add `aria-current="step"` for the current stop.
- Hotspot cards use `role="dialog"` without moving focus; `role="region"` with a label fits a non-modal card.
- No `<h1>` while touring; numbers use hardcoded `toLocaleString("en-US")`.
- Reduced motion is handled reasonably.

## Notes from the build

- Maxime's latest commit on `main` removes the explore page's footer, which held the whole-site Sources and Credits lists. Every photo and model still carries its own credit, but there is no longer one place listing them all.
